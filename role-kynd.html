Working in a team

This was my first time working in an engineering team outside university. I had used agile on university projects, so standups and retrospectives were not new in form — but running them with a team of working engineers turned out to be a different thing from running them with coursemates.

Daily standups were where that showed most. When everyone in the room has their own work to get back to, being concise stops being a courtesy and becomes the point: what I did, what I am doing next, what is blocking me. Learning to judge what actually needed saying to the whole team, and what was better picked up in a conversation afterwards, was probably the most useful habit I took away.

Retrospectives were the other half of it — looking back honestly at what had and had not worked, as a team rather than individually. At the end of the internship I presented what I had built to colleagues, which meant explaining the work to people who had not been inside it with me.
